# Career Paths and Research Opportunities

Robotics offers diverse paths: pure research in SLAM and learning, system integration at scale, deep specialization in hardware, or full-stack work at startups. This section covers the landscape.

Read it in roughly this order. [Job Roles](job-roles-in-robotics.md) tells you what the work actually is, [Getting Hired](getting-hired.md) tells you how to get it, [Companies Hiring](companies-hiring-for-robotics.md) tells you who is doing it, and [Conferences and Journals](conferences-and-journals.md) plus [Leading Institutes](leading-institutes.md) cover the research side.
